Our Story

Built out of frustration with how software was being sold.

Nautilus Code was founded in Katunayake after watching too many local businesses pay large sums for websites that looked impressive but generated zero business value — platforms that couldn't be maintained, didn't convert visitors, and broke within 6 months.

We started with a simple belief: software should solve real operational problems, not just look good on a portfolio. Every system we build is measured against one question — did it save the client time, money, or both?

Today, Nautilus Code operates as a lean, senior-only engineering team — delivering enterprise-grade systems to ambitious businesses across Sri Lanka and internationally.
